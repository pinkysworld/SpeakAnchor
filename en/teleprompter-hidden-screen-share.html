Teleprompter during screen sharing: how do you keep it hidden?

You want to read a script in a video call or recording while your audience sees only your slides. There are several ways, and each has limits.

Why this is a problem

When you share your whole screen, the video service shows everything on it. A window with your script would be visible to everyone. Teams, Zoom and most other services have no teleprompter of their own.

Option 1: share only the window, not the screen

If you pick just the window with your slides, everything else stays private, including the script next to it. The downside: when you switch programs, what the others see changes. And some setups (presentation mode, several windows) are awkward this way.

Option 2: put the script on a second monitor you do not share

Share only monitor 1 and put your script or notes on monitor 2. That is reliable and needs no extra software. You need a second screen, and your eyes move sideways away from the camera.

Option 3: a prompter window that Windows leaves out of screen capture

Windows has a window setting that keeps a window out of screen captures and screen sharing. A prompter that uses it can sit right under the webcam while the audience still sees only the slides. This works only with programs that set it.

Where the limits are

  • Projectors and mirrored monitors show whatever is on the mirrored picture. Put the script on a monitor that is not mirrored.
  • A camera pointed at your screen will of course see the prompter too.
  • Never rely on it blindly: do a test call with a second device or a test recording and check what actually arrives.

Frequently asked questions

Does my audience see the prompter in Teams?

That depends on the method. If you share only a window or use a second monitor you do not share, it stays private. With a prompter window that Windows keeps out of capture, it depends on the program. Test it first.

Does this work for recordings too?

For programs that keep their window out of screen capture, yes. Again: make a short test recording first.

Do I need a second monitor?

No. A second monitor is one option, not the only one.
