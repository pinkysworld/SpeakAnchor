Teleprompter for Windows: what to look for

There are teleprompters in the browser, as open-source programs and as Windows apps. Which one fits depends on what you need it for. These points help you choose.

What do you need it for?

Video recording with a mirror glass needs different things than a Teams call where you want to read notes. Write down your main use case first.

Checklist

  • Hidden when sharing? If the audience must not see the prompter, check that the app explicitly supports it and test with a second device.
  • Speed and control: Can you fine-tune the speed, and can the text start or pause by key even when another app is in front?
  • Clicker or phone: Can you advance from a distance?
  • Import: Can you bring in your texts from TXT, Word or PDF?
  • Mirroring and fullscreen: Important if you use prompter glass or a second monitor.
  • Privacy: Do your scripts stay on your computer, does the app need an account?
  • Pricing model: Free with limits, subscription or one-time purchase? Check current terms in the store.
  • Try first: Test the app before you pay.

Free or paid?

Free browser and open-source teleprompters are enough for many recordings. They are often less geared to video calls or to reading unseen while sharing. For paid apps, look at the pricing model and the trial.

Frequently asked questions

Which Windows version do I need for SpeakAnchor?

Windows 10 version 2004 or later, or Windows 11, on x64 or ARM64.

Do I need an account for a teleprompter?

Not necessarily. It depends on the app. SpeakAnchor needs no account and works offline.

Can I import Word or PDF files?

In SpeakAnchor yes: TXT, Markdown, Word and PDF with a text layer. Scanned PDFs without a text layer cannot be read.
